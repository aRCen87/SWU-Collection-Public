# Home artwork

The Badges and Tournois & Méta menu illustrations are generated raster assets,
matching the existing cinematic navy/gold artwork. Both are displayed with the
same bitmap renderer and text-protection gradient as the other home buttons.
The labels remain native Android text.

Final assets:
- `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/home_badges_art.webp`
- `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/home_meta_art.webp`

Created with the built-in image generation tool, using `home_decks_art.webp` and
`home_trades_art.webp` as style references. Generated originals were converted
to 1000-pixel-wide WebP at quality 90 for the Android resources.

## Prompt specifications

Shared direction: a single wide 2.55:1 illustration; realistic cinematic
collectible-card fantasy rendering, midnight blue starfield, warm aged gold,
tangible dimensional metal, restrained blue rim light, tiny scratches and
reflections. Subjects in the central/right area, dark negative space at left,
all objects within the frame and readable at thumbnail size. No text, letters,
numerals, watermark, UI, outside border or mockup; no cartoon or flat vector icon.

Badges subject: three physical achievement medals/pins with embossed five-point
stars, a larger gold medal in front, smaller silver and bronze medals behind,
navy ribbon tails, subtle blue planetary glow and golden dust near the base.

Tournois & Méta subject: a gold tournament trophy cup with sculpted handles,
embossed five-point star and navy pedestal. Two upright fanned collectible cards
behind it, gold rims, one blue planet and science-fiction spires, the other amber
planet and cloaked silhouette. Brushed gold texture, dramatic cinematic light,
starfield and blue planetary glow.

## 0.8.14 card identity

Scanner, Extensions and Mes decks retain their navy/gold illustration subjects.
Their generic compass-star card-back/booster marks now use the gold Star Wars
Unlimited wordmark. Edited with the built-in image generation tool using the
existing assets and an official card-back logo reference; exported as 1000-pixel
